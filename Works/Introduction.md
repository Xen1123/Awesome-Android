<h1 align=center>Scripts For Androids</h1>
<h2 align=center>For The Power User</h2>

### Prerequisites

- Have a PC or another Android device with Termux's Python environment installed.
    - If you have a PC, go to [Python's Website](https://www.python.org) and download Python for your device OR install it from your terminal.

- Use `python --version` or `python3 --version`, make sure you have either Python 3.13 or 3.14 installed.

- A USB cable that can connect either your 2 Android devices or your PC -> Android

### Content

- Python scripts for various needs
